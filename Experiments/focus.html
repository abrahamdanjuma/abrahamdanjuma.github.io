The 60-Minute Myth

I try to work in 60-minute blocks. To be honest, I don’t always hit it. Some days I get distracted after ten minutes, and other days I’m too deep in a problem to notice the timer ended.

But having this milestone—the "The Block"—helps me reset. It’s not about being perfect; it’s just a reminder to come up for air. When I actually follow it, my head feels a lot clearer by the end of the day.
